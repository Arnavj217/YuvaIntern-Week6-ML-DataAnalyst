# Week 6 Research Notes — Public Agricultural Data & Strategic Context

## Government crop-production data
The Government of India Open Government Data (OGD) Platform provides a district-wise, season-wise crop production resource. The resource describes crop area and production at district/crop/season/year granularity and is maintained under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. The resource page reports an update on 16 October 2024.

Source:
https://data.gov.in/resource/district-wise-season-wise-crop-production-statistics-1997

## Rainfall data
The Government of India OGD Rainfall in India catalog provides month-wise all-India rainfall and sub-division-wise rainfall/departure information and includes historical resources from IMD.

Source:
https://data.gov.in/catalog/rainfall-india

## Agricultural market data / digital market infrastructure
e-NAM is described by its official portal as a pan-India electronic trading portal that networks existing APMC mandis into a unified national market. The platform's stated mission includes transparent auction-based price discovery, quality-based trade and timely online payment.

Source:
https://enam.gov.in/

## Strategic interpretation
The sources demonstrate that an agribusiness analytics platform can combine production, weather and market information. Actual integration requires matching geography, time period, commodity definitions, data permissions, latency and data quality.

## Important evidence note
Prior internship numerical analyses used synthetic demonstration datasets. Those results are useful for methodology demonstration, not as official estimates of agricultural performance. Future operational deployment should validate the complete pipeline on authoritative data.
